# Delegation matrix

Which of your recurring tasks should you hand to AI, and how closely must you check the result? Two questions decide, per task (not per job). Use plausible tasks (own work or a realistic target role). Describe the tasks only, never real company data.

## The two axes

- **Cost of an undetected error:** what happens if a mistake slips through? Who sees the output, what decision does it drive, can it be undone? *Low:* internal, reversible. *High:* customers, money, legal, people.
- **Cost of checking:** what does it take to verify the output? Is there a source to compare against, do you have the expertise, how long does it take? *Cheap:* a source to compare against, your own expertise, minutes. *Expensive:* no source, judgement calls, combining subtle details.

## The four zones

| | **Checking is cheap** | **Checking is expensive** |
|:--|:--|:--|
| **Undetected error costs little** | **1 · Delegate, spot-check** (e.g. meeting agenda draft, reformatting notes) | **3 · Sparring partner:** ideas and alternatives; accept imperfection, do not verify everything (e.g. slogan ideas, interview questions) |
| **Undetected error costs a lot** | **2 · Delegate and verify every claim against the source** (e.g. status report, customer reply with order data) | **4 · Keep, or split** into sub-tasks that fall into zones 1–3 (e.g. performance review, negotiation strategy, credit decision) |

This matrix is a course heuristic built from the field experiments in S01 and S04, not a validated instrument.

## Worth a skill?

A task is a skill candidate if it is **frequent** (weekly or more) and has the **same structure each time** (standardised input and output). Then estimate the break-even:

```text
uses to break even = (build minutes + upkeep minutes) / (manual minutes − AI minutes incl. checking)
```

Example: 60 minutes to build, 30 minutes by hand, 12 minutes with AI including checking → 60 / (30 − 12) ≈ 3.3 uses, so a weekly task breaks even in about 3.3 weeks. If AI with checking is not faster than by hand, the skill never breaks even on time (it may still pay off on quality). Value in euros follows in S10.
