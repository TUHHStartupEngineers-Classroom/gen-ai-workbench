# Use-case scouting (capstone milestone)

Individual work. Your capstone team (formed in S06) builds a GenAI prototype for one use case, and in S10 writes the business case for it. Here you scout two candidates.

A personal skill helps one person. A capstone use case runs as a process: more volume, several users, a data source, and someone has to judge the output. Good candidates have high volume, a clear input and output, quality you can evaluate, and data you can get (synthetic or public only). If you cannot say how you would evaluate the output, it is not a capstone use case yet. Avoid "a chatbot for everything" and "replace a department".

Be specific: numbers, a named evaluation method, a named data source.
