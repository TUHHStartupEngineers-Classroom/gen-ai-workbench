# Nordhafen Logistik GmbH: Travel Expense Policy (excerpt)

Version 4.2, valid from 1 January 2026. Owner: Controlling (Katrin Vogel). Synthetic document for teaching.

## 1. Scope

This policy applies to all business trips of employees of Nordhafen Logistik GmbH, Hamburg. Controlling audits submitted expenses every quarter. Amounts that break a rule are **not reimbursed** until the employee provides a justification or approval ("amount at risk").

## 2. Currency conversion

Expenses in foreign currency are converted into EUR with the **fixed policy rates** below. Do not use daily market rates.

| Currency | Policy rate (Q3 2026) |
|:---------|:----------------------|
| USD | 1 USD = 0.86 EUR |
| GBP | 1 GBP = 1.17 EUR |

All caps in this policy are in EUR and apply to the converted amount. Round converted amounts to the cent.

## 3. Hotel

Hotel costs are reimbursed up to the following cap **per night**, including breakfast. The expense line shows the total for the stay and the number of nights.

| City | Cap per night (EUR) |
|:-----|--------------------:|
| Berlin | 150 |
| Cologne | 140 |
| Düsseldorf | 140 |
| Frankfurt | 150 |
| Hamburg | 140 |
| Munich | 160 |
| London | 210 |
| New York | 260 |
| Chicago | 220 |
| All other cities | 120 |

Amount at risk: the part of the stay above cap × nights.

## 4. Meals (per diem)

Meals are claimed as one line per day. The daily cap depends on the country:

| Country | Daily meal cap (EUR) |
|:--------|---------------------:|
| Germany (DE) | 40 |
| United Kingdom (UK) | 50 |
| United States (US) | 55 |

Amount at risk: the part of the day's meals above the cap.

## 5. Receipts

Every expense line above **EUR 50** (after conversion) requires a receipt. Without a receipt the full line is at risk.

## 6. Weekends

Expenses dated on a Saturday or Sunday are reimbursed only with prior approval by the line manager. Approval is documented in the comment field with the words "weekend approved". Without it the full line is at risk.

## 7. Duplicate submissions

Each expense may be submitted once. A line with the same employee, date, category, merchant, and amount as an earlier line is a duplicate; the full duplicate line is at risk.

## 8. Travel class

Rail: any class. Flights: economy class. (Not part of this quarter's audit scope.)
